Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Mark Job Application Agent is a personal, owner-operated job-search automation service. It is used for the owner's engineering job search in Germany. This notice describes how the application accesses and uses Google user data.

Google information processed

  • Google Sheets: Job-tracker rows, search keywords and locations, application preferences, autofill rules, and application status fields.
  • Gmail: Messages and relevant metadata processed to identify job application confirmations, interview or rejection notices, and verification codes from job-application portals. Incoming messages may be examined to determine whether they are relevant.
  • Google Drive: Owner-approved CVs, cover-letter templates, and generated application packages synchronized by the automation.

Purposes and limited use

Google user information is used only to run the requested job-search, application-document, status-tracking, and authorized notification functions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to build advertising profiles. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

The job tracker and related documents remain within the owner's Google account unless intentionally copied or synchronized to the owner's private Oracle Cloud runtime. The n8n workflow service and database may keep processing state, job records, application-status information, and operational logs needed for the workflow. The public informational website does not collect Google account data.

Sharing and disclosures

Job-application information and approved CV/cover-letter documents may be provided to an employer's application portal when the owner authorizes application preparation or submission. The workflow necessarily processes information through its configured providers, including Google services, Oracle Cloud, and n8n. We do not sell Google user data or grant advertising companies access to it.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained as needed for the owner's ongoing job-search and recovery workflows, or until the owner deletes them from Google Sheets, Google Drive, the runtime, or its backups. There is no fixed automatic retention period stated for all categories. The owner controls the Google account and the private runtime, including removing generated files or stopping the automation.

Security

Application profiles, authentication credentials, and browser sessions are intended to remain in private runtime storage, outside public source code. Only authorized access should be granted to the runtime. The application does not intentionally publish private Google account content on this site.

Account access and revocation

Google access can be revoked at Google Account — Third-party connections. Revoking access may stop the automated tracker and email functions. The owner can also disable the workflows and request deletion of data in services the owner controls.

Contact

For questions about access, privacy, or deletion, contact GitHub profile.
